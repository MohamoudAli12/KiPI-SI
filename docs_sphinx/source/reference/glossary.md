# Glossary

## Terms used in KiPI-SI

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:class: kipi-table

* - Term
  - Meaning
* - PI
  - Power integrity: delivering clean, correct supply voltage.
* - PDN
  - Power distribution network: regulators, copper, vias and capacitors between a supply and its loads.
* - VRM
  - Voltage regulator module: any regulator or supply.
* - IR drop
  - Voltage lost to current flowing through copper resistance.
* - Net group
  - Nets solved together because series links join them.
* - Remote sense
  - A VRM regulating the voltage at a point other than its output.
* - PTH
  - Plated through hole (pads with plated drills).
* - ESR / ESL
  - Equivalent series resistance / inductance of a part.
* - DCR
  - DC resistance of a series part.
* - Target impedance
  - The highest PDN impedance that keeps supply ripple within tolerance for the expected load steps.
* - Anti-resonance
  - An impedance peak where one capacitor bank's inductance resonates with another's capacitance.
* - TCR
  - Temperature coefficient of resistance.
```
